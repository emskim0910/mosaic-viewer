Monolithic, Optimized & Scalable Additively Integrated Cold-Gas (MOSAIC) Thruster

A low-cost, monolithically integrated cold gas thruster (CGT) for CubeSat-class small satellites, targeting a 1U form factor. The MOSAIC thruster addresses the mass penalty and leak risks of conventional propulsion solutions by additively manufacturing the pressure vessel, feed manifolds, and nozzle as a single AlSi10Mg structure.

Current state-of-the-art CubeSat propulsion systems cost more than US$100,000 per unit, pricing out university research groups, independent labs, and early-stage commercial operators. At US$3,000 per unit, MOSAIC achieves a 97% reduction in unit cost through architectural simplification that prioritizes reliability and market accessibility. Ranked 3rd among 95 key shortfalls identified by NASA's technology taxonomy, small spacecraft propulsion is a critical and underserved capability gap.

NASA Space Grant Brown University Open Research

Three Differentiators

  • Low cost: $3K per unit vs $100K+ industry standard (97% reduction)
  • Monolithic integration: AlSi10Mg pressure vessel doubles as the CubeSat's load-bearing chassis via LPBF/SLM, eliminating deadweight and leak points
  • Scalability: standardized internal manifolds scale across 1U, 2U and 3U without redesign

Development Roadmap

Development Phases

  • I
    CAD: 1U, 2U & 3U Configurations
    Monolithic model integrating pressure vessel, internal manifolds, and nozzle in a single continuous print. Geometry designed to scale for 2U and 3U configurations.
    Status · Oct 2026: vessel Rev5 complete (the model in the viewer); nozzle and feed integration in work.
  • II
    FEA & Topology Optimization
    Remove surplus material from non-load-bearing regions while maintaining a 2× safety factor under simulated launch loads up to 10G.
    Status · Oct 2026: pressure sizing in progress across five vessel revisions (wall, junction, corner and hole models); SolidWorks checks on Rev4 and Rev5; launch loads not yet run.
  • III
    CFD: Internal Manifold Optimization
    Nonlinear CFD to optimize curvilinear manifolds, minimize stagnation points, and characterize the discharge coefficient of the feed path.
  • IV
    AM Fabrication & COTS Integration
    Additive manufacture the prototype and integrate all commercial subsystems into the completed assembly.
  • V
    Leak Testing & Cold-Flow Testing
    Validate the fluid and feed system performance with a nontoxic inert fluid.
  • VI
    Data Analysis & Presentation
    Compile findings from leak and cold-flow tests and summarize lessons learned.

Technology Evolution Strategy

Phase 1: Short-term
Drag-Compensation & Deorbiting
MOSAIC is a drag-compensation and deorbiting service for VLEO CubeSats and constellations, in which electrical propulsion systems are expensive overkill.
Phase 2: Medium-term
Warm-Gas Thruster
Add a cartridge heat exchanger upstream of the nozzle to raise Isp beyond cold-gas limits.
Phase 3: Long-term
Water Resistojet
Transition to steam-based thrust benchmarked against Pale Blue's non-ITAR PBR-20. Water propellant eliminates pressure storage concerns and improves Isp further.
